Treatments for different dental needs

An overview of common treatments. Please confirm availability and suitability with the clinic.

Dental Implants

A small post placed in the jaw supports a replacement tooth. Ask whether implants suit your situation.

Learn more about Dental Implants

Root Canal Treatment

Treats an infected or inflamed nerve inside a tooth so the natural tooth can be kept.

Learn more about Root Canal Treatment

Braces & Clear Aligners

Brackets and wires, or a series of clear removable trays, gradually move teeth into place.

Learn more about Braces & Clear Aligners

Teeth Whitening

Professional lightening of tooth shade, planned after a check of your teeth and gums.

Learn more about Teeth Whitening

Smile Designing

Planning changes to the shape, shade or alignment of teeth before any treatment starts.

Learn more about Smile Designing

Crowns & Bridges

A crown protects a weakened tooth; a bridge replaces a missing tooth using its neighbours.

Learn more about Crowns & Bridges
