Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Use Book an Appointment on any page. Fill in your name, mobile number and preferred date, and your request opens in WhatsApp. Press Send, and the clinic will contact you to confirm a time.

What are the clinic timings?

The current reference timings are Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am–12:00 pm and 6:30–9:00 pm. These are provisional, so please confirm with the clinic before visiting.

Where exactly is the clinic?

No. 24, Navalar Main Road, Devaraj Nagar, near Shankar Narayanan Temple, Saligramam, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600093. Use Get directions to open the location in Google Maps.

Which treatments are available?

The clinic lists general consultations, implants, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, fillings, extractions, dentures and more. See the Treatments page and confirm availability when you book.

Is my information stored by this website?

No. The booking form only prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. Nothing is stored by the website, and nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp. The Chat with Us help desk works entirely in your browser and does not send or store your messages.

What should I bring to my first visit?

If you have them, bring previous dental records or X-rays, a list of the medicines you take, and details of any medical conditions or allergies. Arriving a few minutes early gives time to share this with the dentist.

Are treatment fees listed on the website?

No. Fees are not published here because they depend on an examination and your treatment plan. Please ask the clinic when you book.

Is my appointment confirmed when I send the form?

No. The form prepares a request in WhatsApp. Your appointment is confirmed only when the clinic contacts you.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

If you have severe facial swelling, swelling spreading towards the eye or neck, difficulty breathing or swallowing, bleeding that will not stop, a high fever with dental pain, or a facial injury, seek urgent medical care straight away at the nearest hospital emergency department, or call 108 (ambulance) or 112 (emergency). Do not wait for an appointment request.

Is the Chat with Us assistant a dentist?

No. It is an automated help desk with set answers about the clinic and general dental care. It cannot diagnose, prescribe, quote fees or book appointments.

Prefer to book now?

Send your preferred date and time. The clinic will contact you to confirm.
